LAB 0

Getting Started with Copilot Cowork

5–10 minutesBeginnerNo custom Skill or Plugin

Scenario. You are a market intelligence analyst at a technology consulting company exploring AI consulting opportunities in the UAE. Leadership needs an overview of the market, competitors, opportunities, risks and recommendations.

Hands-on

  1. Sign in to Microsoft 365 Copilot using your lab account.
  2. Open Cowork → New task.
  3. Copy the prompt below and submit it.
  4. Review the plan, if displayed, and respond to any necessary approvals or clarifications.
  5. Inspect the generated report and its sources.
I am a Market Intelligence Analyst at a technology consulting company exploring AI consulting opportunities in the UAE. Prepare a structured market intelligence report covering: (1) UAE AI services market overview, (2) five relevant AI consulting competitors, (3) emerging opportunities, (4) market-entry risks, and (5) recommendations for leadership. Cite important claims and distinguish verified facts from assumptions.

Completion check

Reflect: What if your team needs this report for different Geographies every week, in the same format, without rewriting a detailed prompt?
LAB 1

Market Intelligence with Cowork Skills

10–15 minutesBeginnerReady-to-use SkillNo YAML editing

Scenario. Leadership now wants a consistent weekly market and competitor intelligence brief for different geographies. Use an existing Skill that packages the research method, report structure, citations and recommendations.

Hands-on

  1. Download the ready-to-use Market Intelligence Brief Skill using the button below. No configuration edits are needed.
  2. Open Copilot → Cowork → Plugins.
  3. Select Add → Upload, then choose the downloaded Skill file.
  4. Confirm that market-intelligence-brief is listed as an added capability.
  5. Go to Cowork → New task and submit the short prompt below.
  6. When Cowork asks which geography to cover, enter Singapore for this exercise. Provide the reporting window or sources if requested, then review the report.
Download Market Intelligence SKILL.mdDownload the Skill file before uploading it to Cowork. If the download is blocked in preview, use the separately provided Skill file.
Generate this week's Market Intelligence Brief using the market-intelligence-brief Skill.

Completion check

Key takeaway: Lab 0 researches the UAE using a detailed prompt. Lab 1 uses the reusable Skill for Singapore with a short request, showing how the same methodology can be applied across markets. A short prompt does not replace reliable source data.
LAB 3

Build a Market Intelligence App with Cowork

15–20 minutesIntermediateNo codeDemo data, saved in your browser

Scenario. Leadership wants more than a report. They want a simple tool to track AI market developments across UAE, Singapore, the UK and India, and to follow up on recommended actions. Use Cowork to build an interactive app from a single prompt.

Hands-on

  1. Go to Cowork → New task.
  2. Copy the prompt below and submit it.
    Build a single-page "AI Market Intelligence Monitor" app for Contoso Consulting leadership to track AI market developments across geographies. TABS: 1. Dashboard: 4 headline counts (trends, competitor moves, high-impact risks, open actions), one bar chart of records by geography, and lists of opportunities and risks. 2. Intelligence: one searchable list of competitors, trends, opportunities and risks, each showing geography, confidence (High/Medium/Low) and impact. 3. Action Tracker: a table of recommended actions where I can change priority, owner, due date and status (Not started / In progress / Blocked / Completed). 4. Add Intelligence: a form to add or edit a record, plus a field to add a new geography. A geography filter and a search box at the top apply to every tab. DEMO DATA: realistic made-up data for UAE, Singapore, UK and India. For each geography: 3 competitors (fictional names), 3 trends, 2 opportunities, 2 risks and 3 recommended actions, all in a single reporting period (Q4 2026). All changes must save in the browser and still be there after a page refresh. BUILD RULES (follow exactly): - Build immediately. Do not ask me any questions; use sensible defaults. - Store all data in browser localStorage only. Do not use SharePoint, Dataverse, Fabric, connectors, external APIs or AI features, and do not offer to save the data anywhere else. Keep it local. - One page only, with 4 tabs. No extra pages or routes. - Keep the code compact: one data file and one page file. - Do not publish the app yet.
  3. Wait for the app to open in the live preview. It can take 5–7 minutes to finish setting up the app.
  4. On Dashboard, review the four headline counts, the bar chart by geography, and the opportunity and risk lists.
  5. Set the geography filter to India, then type pricing in the search box. Move between tabs and confirm the filter and search apply to every tab. Then clear both.
  6. On Action Tracker, change the status of one action to Blocked and change its owner.
  7. On Add Intelligence, add the geography Saudi Arabia, then add one new trend for it.
  8. Refresh the preview and confirm your changes are still there.
  9. Publish the app: in the same Cowork task, send Publish the app. Approve the request if prompted, then open the app link that Cowork returns.
  10. Share the app: in the same task, send Share the app with user.r0ur9fij@copilotstudiotraining.onmicrosoft.com. Approve the sharing request if prompted, and confirm that Cowork reports the app as shared.

Completion check

Key takeaway: Labs 0 and 1 turn research into a report. Lab 3 turns the same market intelligence scenario into a working tool that leadership can filter, search and update. All data in this lab is fictional demo data and is saved only in your browser.
